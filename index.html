About Me

I'm Matanda Mabomba Exaucé—a refugee-background professional, pedagogue, and community builder based in Nakivale, Uganda. I work at the intersection of education, technology, and opportunity creation. My focus: I help young people—especially refugees and marginalized communities—navigate barriers to education and employment. For 2+ years, I've mentored refugee youth on education access and helped 5+ young people secure $25,000+ in scholarships. Currently, I'm building this impact through technology. I'm learning web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) through the DigiPass programme and studying at University of the People. I'm also offering administrative and back-office support services while I develop my technical skills. My background: I'm a trained pedagogue with a Diplôme d'État in General Pedagogy from the DRC. I completed the Kepler-Iteme Foundation Programme and the RETI Programme at Bishop Stuart University (Mastercard Foundation). My lived experience as a refugee and my work in resource-constrained, multicultural communities deeply inform my approach to everything I build. My faith: I'm an active Christian—serving in evangelism, intercession, and preaching—believing that technology, education, and faith together can transform lives and communities. Currently: Building my web development portfolio while creating real impact through mentorship, education support, and digital skills training in my community.
