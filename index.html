About Me

I am Matanda Mabomba Exaucé — a web developer, youth advocate, community builder, and servant of God based in Kampala, Uganda. I build digital tools and learning spaces that open doors for young people who are often left out of mainstream opportunities. I completed web development and ICT training through the RETI Programme at Bishop Stuart University, supported by the Mastercard Foundation, and I am currently a DigiPass Fellow advancing my digital skills further. I am the founder of Rhythm of Hope, a learning initiative that uses music, mentorship, and digital skills training to build confidence and resilience in youth. As a drummer and educator, I believe creativity and technology together are powerful tools for change. My faith is central to everything I do. I serve actively in evangelism, intercession, and preaching — believing that technology, education, and the Gospel can work together to transform lives and communities. I have worked in multicultural and resource-constrained environments — including refugee settlement contexts — supporting learners through English facilitation, mentorship, and community engagement. That experience shapes everything I build.

Currently building my portfolio as a web developer and digital creator while creating real impact in my community.
